Summer Learning in Full Swing

Drop-ins are underway & your summer & fall PD lineup

Kevin Westwood  ·  Tuesday, August 4, 2026  ·  3 min read

01

August — Breaking Barriers: Depression

Presented by Shannon McCracken, Career Services Consultant.

Depression can be a significant barrier to employment, affecting motivation, confidence, focus, and job search success. Join Shannon for this engaging one-hour session designed for frontline employment service professionals to deepen your understanding of depression and its impact on job seekers.

Learn practical strategies to recognize signs of depression, have supportive conversations, reduce stigma, and help job seekers move toward their employment goals with confidence and dignity.

03

Occupational Therapy Assessments

Are you or a job seeker you support unsure about their physical or cognitive abilities? Would you like more information on these factors to ensure the job seeker's path to employment is a good fit? Our Occupational Therapist can complete Functional Capacity Evaluations (FCEs) and cognitive assessments to provide you with more information — as well as consultations to assist job seekers in managing injury/illness symptoms, discuss accommodations, and more!

Send in a referral to The Hub's Occupational Therapist any time.

Submit an OT referral

04

Career Exploration

Working with a job seeker who isn't sure what direction to take? Employment service professionals are invited to request a consultation with our Career Services Consultant, Shannon McCracken. Together you can talk through where the job seeker is at and decide which career exploration assessment would be the most useful next step.

Two assessments are available through The Hub @PATH, and both help job seekers gain insight into their interests, strengths, and potential career pathways.

Jackson Career Explorer (JCE)

The Jackson Career Explorer is a comprehensive assessment that helps job seekers identify occupations aligned with their interests, skills, work values, and personality traits. With personalized career matches and detailed occupational information, the JCE supports informed career planning and decision-making.

Ashland Interest Assessment

The Ashland Interest Assessment helps job seekers explore career options by identifying their strongest vocational interests. Based on established career interest themes, it provides practical insights into occupations and work environments that may be a good fit, making it a valuable tool for career exploration and employment planning.

To get started, email Shannon directly and she will follow up with you.
